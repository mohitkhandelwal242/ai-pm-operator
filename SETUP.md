# ✦ AI-PM Operator

You have successfully installed the **AI-PM Operator**! This is a production-grade AI operations toolkit that connects to your company's real tools (Jira, Google Analytics, GSC, mobile stores) and gives you an autonomous co-pilot directly inside Claude Code.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (pull → set up → use)

### 1. Drop it into your project
Pull/copy this package into your repo root (or a fresh folder) and open it in Claude Code:
```bash
claude
```

### 2. It sets itself up for *your* business
On first launch, the Operator detects there's no config yet and runs **onboarding automatically**. It will:
- Look at your repo to infer what you're building (stack, product, platforms).
- Ask a few quick questions — business type, key metrics, competitors, tools.
- Write your `business.json` (business profile), `.env` (credentials), and `team.json` (roster).
- Tailor every skill to your business — your metrics, your competitors, your domain.

Just say **"set up"** (or run `/operator-onboarding`) if it doesn't start on its own. Prefer a guided script for credentials? Run:
```bash
python3 tools/setup-wizard.py
```
Re-run anytime your business or tools change: `/operator-onboarding --reconfigure`.

### 3. Run your first workflow
Once configured, try:
- `Run daily scrum master sweep` or `/scrum` — audits Kanban health, flags stale/blocked issues.
- `Generate a weekly plan` or `/weekly-plan` — sprint plan based on capacity.
- `Write a PRD for [feature]` or `/prd-generator` — drafts a complete spec.
- `/competitor-tracker` — first competitive pulse on the competitors you named.

> Nothing here is tied to any one industry. A PM at a B2B SaaS, a consumer app, a marketplace, or an e-commerce brand all get a toolkit shaped to their business via `business.json`.

## 🔒 Security & Data Privacy

- **Credentials stay local**: Your API keys and tokens (`.env`), and your team roster (`team.json`) **never leave your machine** — they're used only to talk directly to your own tool endpoints (Atlassian Jira, Google APIs, etc.). We never receive, store, or train on them.
- **Product analytics (disclosed)**: AI-PM Operator sends the maker a small, **non-sensitive business profile summary** at install and as a periodic usage heartbeat — company/product name, business type, industry, revenue model, platforms, website domain, your headline metrics, an anonymous install id, and trial/licensed status. This is base64-encoded and sent over HTTPS to help us understand what kinds of businesses use the product and to support licensing. **We never send your credentials, Jira/Confluence tokens, `team.json` (names/emails/IDs), competitor names, or your `context_notes`.** Opt out anytime by setting `OPERATOR_TELEMETRY=off` in `.env`.
- **Git Safeguards**: The installer automatically updates your `.gitignore` file to ensure `.env`, `business.json`, and `team.json` are never committed to your code repositories.
